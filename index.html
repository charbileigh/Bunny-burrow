How background focus works

Start your session before switching apps. Some browsers keep sound playing with the screen locked; others pause it. Lock-screen playback controls appear where supported.

If you fully close the app, audio and timed bells stop. Your session is saved, and the timer and bunny growth catch up when you reopen it. Offline mode saves the app’s files—it does not grant permission to run after closure.

Your own little burrow

Keep Bunny Burrow on your home screen and open it as an app.

iPhone or iPad

Open this page in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Android

Open this page in Chrome. Use the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. The wording may vary.

Open the hosted HTTPS link first. Offline mode is ready when the app says “Ready offline”. Your timer, choices and bunnies are saved on this device. Reopening catches up with elapsed time. Fully closing the app prevents sounds and alarms until it is opened again.
